How we operate

Owned end to end, so nothing slips between hands.

— 01

Extraction & processing

Our own sites and equipment, run by crews who answer to us — not a chain of subcontractors. Output is screened, assayed and graded on site.

  • Open-pit & surface mining
  • On-site crushing & screening
  • SANAS-accredited assay
— 02

Scrap recovery

Licensed yards take in copper and aluminium, sort to ISRI grade, and consolidate to mill-ready loads. Fair weigh, documented, traceable.

  • Second-hand metals permit
  • ISRI-graded sorting
  • Container consolidation

Logistics & offtake

From pit or yard to port or plant. We structure offtake around your volume, terms and timeline — domestic delivery or export documentation included.

  • Export documentation
  • LC / TT terms
